# Lume — moeda ilustrada

Ferramenta: image_gen integrada. Arte original em lume-currency-v1.png; versão WebP transparente utilizada no jogo. Três células horizontais: moeda principal, saída/compra vermelha, entrada/venda verde.

## Prompt de geração

Create one production sprite atlas for a beautiful fictional space-game currency named Lume. Exactly THREE equal square cells in a horizontal strip, ratio 3:1. Each cell contains ONE same-design coin, centered, completely isolated on true transparent background with generous 15 percent margins. No text, letters, numbers, labels, UI frames, backgrounds, stacks, or additional objects. Style: lavishly detailed realistic cartoon RPG inventory illustration, sculpted metal, premium hand-painted science-fiction object, not vector, not flat icon. Coin design: a thick circular titanium and aged champagne-gold coin, elegant slightly scalloped outer rim with precision etched concentric orbit lines, small mechanical facets and beautiful edge highlights; a clearly recognizable luminous four-point stellar diamond emblem in the central inset, encircled by a single delicate orbital ellipse. Same gentle three-quarter angle in all cells, face mostly facing viewer for readability at 48 pixels, visible metal thickness at lower right. Distinctive bold silhouette, restrained details that survive tiny display. Cell 1 LEFT: master currency coin, rich gold rim, midnight-blue inset and warm ivory stellar light. Cell 2 MIDDLE: purchase/outgoing variant of EXACT SAME coin shape, ruby-red central enamel and soft red luminous star, gold/titanium rim preserved. Cell 3 RIGHT: sale/incoming variant of EXACT SAME coin shape, emerald-green central enamel and soft mint-green luminous star, gold/titanium rim preserved. Warm upper-left highlights, deep grounded metal shading, controlled luminous accents, no exaggerated glow outside silhouettes. Equal scale, same orientation, consistent cell placement, no overlap. Designed to match intricately rendered alien plants, glass resource canisters and brass spaceship tools in a realistic cartoon exploration game.
